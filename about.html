Our Story

Care That Doesn't Change Hands

Most people navigating fertility or a complicated pregnancy describe the same frustration: repeating their story, to a new face, at every stage. Practising as a couple was our answer to that.

Dr. Rohan Patel works in reproductive medicine, IVF and minimal-access gynaecological surgery. Dr. Shreya Patel works in obstetrics, maternal-fetal care and infertility management. Between them, a patient can move from a first fertility consultation through to delivery and afterwards without ever leaving the same two pairs of hands.

  • Complementary specialisms — reproductive medicine and surgery alongside obstetrics and maternal-fetal care.
  • Complete continuity — couples move from fertility care into pregnancy without changing teams.
  • Evidence-based, personalised treatment pathways designed around each couple.

Why a Couple

Two Perspectives. One Care Philosophy.

What practising together actually changes for the people we look after.

Two Perspectives, One Plan

For complex reproductive or high-risk obstetric cases you can have the combined clinical judgement of both specialists, working from a single coordinated plan rather than two separate opinions.

Continuity, Start to Finish

Care doesn't restart when your situation changes. Couples move from fertility treatment into pregnancy and delivery with the same two doctors who already know their history.

Conversations, Not Consultations

Unhurried explanation of what we found, what it means and what your options are — in language that makes sense, with room for the questions you were nervous to ask.

Personalised, Evidence-Based Care

Treatment plans are built around your history, your test results and your priorities, and grounded in current evidence rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.
