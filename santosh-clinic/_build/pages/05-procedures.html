Core Endoscopic Procedures

{{l:scope}}

Upper GI Endoscopy

Gastroscopy · OGD

Examination of the oesophagus, stomach and duodenum. May be advised for persistent reflux, upper abdominal symptoms, difficulty swallowing, vomiting or gastrointestinal bleeding.

{{l:intestine}}

Colonoscopy

Large-intestine examination

May be advised for GI bleeding, persistent change in bowel habits, unexplained anaemia, chronic diarrhoea, inflammatory bowel disease, or appropriate colorectal screening and surveillance.

{{l:search}}

Sigmoidoscopy

Lower-colon examination

A focused endoscopic examination of the lower part of the large intestine, performed in selected clinical situations.

{{l:gall}}

ERCP

Bile duct & pancreas

Specialised endoscopic evaluation and treatment of selected biliary and pancreatic duct disorders.

Before a Procedure

You’ll always know why — and what to expect.
