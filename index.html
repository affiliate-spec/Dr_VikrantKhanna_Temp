Welcome

Two Specialities. One Approach to Care.

Health concerns are often personal, sometimes complex and rarely limited to a single symptom.

At Santosh Gastro Gynae Clinic, we provide specialist consultation in Gastroenterology, Hepatology, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, with an emphasis on careful evaluation, appropriate investigation and clear communication.

Whether you are seeking help for a digestive or liver problem, planning a pregnancy, dealing with a gynaecological concern or navigating changes in women’s health, our aim is to provide thoughtful medical care in a comfortable and approachable environment.

Common Symptoms. Sometimes More Than Meets the Eye.

Acidity, abdominal discomfort, irregular periods, pelvic pain or changes in the menstrual cycle are common experiences. Sometimes they are temporary and uncomplicated. At other times, persistent or recurrent symptoms may be the first indication of an underlying medical condition.

The important step is not to assume — or to worry unnecessarily — but to understand the cause.

How We Work

Different Specialities. The Same Principle of Care.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Understand what is troubling you.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    Look at the symptoms, history, examination and available reports.

  3. 03

    Investigate

    Recommend investigations when they are clinically appropriate.

  4. 04

    Explain

    Help you understand what the findings mean.

  5. 05

    Treat

    Choose treatment according to the diagnosis and your circumstances.

  6. 06

    Follow Up

    Monitor progress and modify the plan when needed.

Good medical care begins with listening and ends with understanding.
