About the Clinic

A Specialist Family Practice, Built on Listening.

Santosh Gastro Gynae Clinic brings together two specialists — one for digestive and liver health, one for women’s health — under one roof in Lucknow.

Our Story

Two Specialities. One Approach to Care.

Good medical care begins with listening and ends with understanding.

For many years, patients in Lucknow have come to Santosh Gastro Gynae Clinic for two kinds of help: problems of the digestive system and liver, and the health concerns that women face through different stages of life.

The clinic is the brand, and both doctors stand behind it equally. Dr. Vikrant Khanna leads Gastroenterology, Hepatology and Endoscopy. Dr. Ritu Khanna leads Obstetrics and Gynaecology.

The clinic is an outpatient consultation practice — specialist consultation, follow-up and scheduled endoscopy, without inpatient beds or admission.

Different specialities, the same principle of care: careful evaluation, appropriate investigation, clear explanation, and follow-up that doesn’t end with a prescription.

Digestive & Liver Health

Understanding your symptoms. Finding the cause.

Dr. Vikrant Khanna is a Gastroenterologist and Hepatologist with more than 26 years of clinical experience. His practice covers the evaluation and management of gastrointestinal, liver, biliary and pancreatic disorders, along with diagnostic and therapeutic gastrointestinal endoscopy.

His approach centres on careful clinical evaluation, appropriate investigation and understanding the underlying problem — rather than simply treating symptoms in isolation.

Women’s Health

Listen. Understand. Explain. Care.

Dr. Ritu Khanna is an obstetrician and gynaecologist with more than 20 years of clinical experience, providing care for women across different stages of life — menstrual and gynaecological health, pregnancy and women’s mid-life health.

Her practice emphasises careful clinical assessment, clear communication and individualised care. Her aim is to create a comfortable environment in which women can discuss their health concerns openly and receive appropriate medical advice.
