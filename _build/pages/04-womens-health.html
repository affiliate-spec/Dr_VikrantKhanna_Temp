Gynaecology

Because women’s health is personal.

Gynaecological problems can affect periods, fertility, relationships, daily activities and emotional wellbeing. Many women live with symptoms because they assume they are “normal” or simply part of being a woman.

Persistent or troublesome symptoms deserve proper evaluation.

Obstetrics

Pregnancy is a journey that deserves careful care.

Pregnancy brings physical, hormonal and emotional changes that are unique to every woman. Regular antenatal care helps monitor the health of both mother and baby, identify potential problems early and provide the right advice throughout.

Every pregnancy is different. Good antenatal care is about understanding that difference.

Adolescent Girls & Menstrual Health

Periods should not be a subject of embarrassment.

The first few years after periods begin can be confusing. A friendly, confidential and age-appropriate consultation helps distinguish normal changes from problems that need evaluation.

  1. {{l:refresh}}

    Irregular periods

    Cycles that are very unpredictable or widely spaced.

  2. {{l:bud}}

    Painful periods

    Menstrual pain that interferes with school, work or everyday activities.

  3. {{l:drop}}

    Heavy bleeding

    Periods that are unusually heavy or prolonged.

  4. {{l:clipboard}}

    Absent periods

    When menstruation does not start at the expected age or stops unexpectedly.

  5. {{l:leaf}}

    PCOS / PCOD

    A common hormonal condition that may affect periods, weight, acne, hair growth and fertility.

{{l:leaf}}

PCOS / PCOD

More than just irregular periods

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a common hormonal and metabolic condition, and it can look very different from one woman to another.

It may be associated with irregular periods, difficulty with ovulation, acne, increased facial or body hair, weight-related problems and metabolic abnormalities.

Management is individualised — lifestyle measures, treatment of menstrual or hormonal problems, and attention to long-term metabolic health.

Menopause

A new phase — not the end of women’s health

The transition around menopause can bring symptoms that affect sleep, mood, energy, periods and day-to-day wellbeing.

  • Irregular periods
  • Hot flushes
  • Night sweats
  • Sleep disturbance
  • Mood changes
  • Vaginal dryness
  • Urinary symptoms
  • Changes in sexual health
  • Bone-health concerns
  • Metabolic & cardiovascular risk

The right approach depends on a woman’s age, symptoms, medical history and individual circumstances.
